About 3D Print Color

Covering full-color and multi-material additive manufacturing — from CMYK voxel control to the materials and machines that make color real.

OUR STORY

Color, Resolved to the Voxel

3D Print Color covers the full spectrum of full-color additive manufacturing — PolyJet, binder jetting, and multi-material systems that assign color and material voxel by voxel, printing photorealistic parts with no painting and no assembly.

Our editorial team follows the machines, the materials, and the color science — from ICC profiles and gamut mapping to the finishing that protects a printed part's color for the long haul.

PURPOSE

Our Mission

To cover full-color AM from the voxel to the finished part with equal depth — the process, the material, and the color science behind them.

Our Vision

To be the authoritative, vendor-independent source for color and multi-material 3D printing — connecting designers and manufacturers to the suppliers pushing color fidelity forward.
